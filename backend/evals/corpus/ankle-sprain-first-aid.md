# First aid for an ankle sprain

What to do in the first two days after twisting an ankle.

## Rest, ice, compression and elevation

- **Rest**: stop the activity and do not walk on it if it hurts.
- **Ice**: 15 to 20 minutes every two or three hours, with a cloth between the
  ice and the skin.
- **Compression**: an elastic bandage, firm but not tight. Loosen it if the
  toes go cold or blue.
- **Elevation**: keep the ankle above the level of the heart when you sit or
  lie down.

## When to see a doctor

If you cannot take four steps, if the bone hurts when you press it, or if the
swelling does not go down after three days, get an X-ray.
