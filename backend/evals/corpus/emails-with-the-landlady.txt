Emails with the landlady

From me, 3 March:
Hi Carmen, a question for when we leave the flat next year. How does the
return of the deposit work?

From Carmen, 4 March:
Hi. On the last day we check the flat together. If everything is in order, I
return the deposit within one month, by bank transfer. If there is any damage,
I take the cost of the repair from it and send you the invoice.

From me, 20 April:
We want to paint the living room light green. Is that allowed?

From Carmen, 21 April:
Yes, you can paint it, as long as the walls are white again when you leave.

From me, 2 November:
The boiler has stopped and there is no hot water.

From Carmen, 2 November:
The boiler is my responsibility. I will send the technician tomorrow morning,
and I pay the repair.
