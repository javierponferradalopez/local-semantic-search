Thank-you email after an interview

Send it the same day, before the end of the afternoon. Short: five or six lines.
One email to each person who interviewed me, not one email to all.

Template:

Subject: Thank you for today's interview

Hi <name>,

Thank you for your time today. I enjoyed the talk about <something specific that
we discussed, for example how the team moved the bookings to queues>.

It made me even more interested in the role. If you need anything else from me,
such as references or a code sample, I will be happy to send it.

Best regards,
<my name>

Do not ask about the decision in this email. If there is no answer after one
week, send one short follow-up.
