# Cactus and succulents on the balcony

The succulents are the only plants that like the south balcony in July.

## Sun and soil

They want full sun, at least six hours a day. Grow them in a gritty soil: half
cactus compost and half pumice or coarse sand, in a terracotta pot that
breathes.

## Water

Soak and dry. Water them well, let the water drain out, and do not water again
until the soil is completely dry: about every three weeks in summer and once a
month in winter. In winter, keep them under cover from the rain.

Too much water makes the leaves soft and see-through, and the stem goes black
at the base. A wrinkled leaf means the plant is thirsty.
