# Wedding timeline

Our checklist from the engagement to the day. The wedding is on Saturday 12
September.

## 12 to 9 months before

- Fix the date and the budget.
- Visit venues and book one. Pay the 20 % deposit to the venue to hold the date.
- Book the photographer.

## 8 to 6 months before

- Send the save-the-date to the guests who travel from far away.
- Choose the dress and the suit.
- Book the band.

## 5 to 3 months before

- Menu tasting at the venue. We chose the menu of 95 euros a head.
- Start the papers at the civil registry.

## 2 months before

- Send the invitations, eight weeks before the day, with the date to answer by.

## The last month

- Final number of guests to the venue, ten days before.
- The seating plan.
- Pay the rest to the venue, the week before.
