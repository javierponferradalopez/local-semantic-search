# Monstera care sheet

The Monstera deliciosa of the living room, and what keeps it healthy.

## Light

Bright, indirect light, one or two metres from a window. Direct sun scorches
the leaves and leaves brown, dry patches. With too little light the new leaves
come out small and without holes.

## Water

Water when the top five centimetres of soil are dry: about once a week in
summer and every two weeks in winter. Pour until water comes out of the
drainage holes, and empty the saucer after ten minutes.

Yellow leaves at the bottom of the plant usually mean too much water. Check
that the pot drains and let the soil dry out more before the next watering.

## Repotting

Repot every two years, in spring, or when roots come out of the drainage
holes. Use a pot only one size bigger, with a chunky soil of bark and perlite.

## Propagation

Cut a stem just below a node that has an aerial root. Put the cutting in a jar
of water in bright light and change the water every week. After three or four
weeks it has roots of five centimetres, and it can go into a small pot.
