# Race day checklist

What I do on the day of a half marathon, after three races of mistakes.

## The night before

Lay out all the kit and pin the bib to the shirt. Dinner is pasta or rice,
nothing new and nothing spicy. Set two alarms.

## Breakfast

Eat three hours before the start, so the stomach is empty at the gun. Eat only
what you tested in a long run: porridge with a banana, or toast with honey, and
one coffee. Drink half a litre of water, then small sips until the start.

## During the race

- Start ten seconds per kilometre slower than the goal pace for the first five
  kilometres.
- Take an energy gel every 45 minutes, always with water from an aid station,
  never with a sports drink.
- Run the tangents of the curves: the measured distance is the shortest line.

## After the finish

Keep walking for ten minutes, drink, and eat something salty within the hour.
