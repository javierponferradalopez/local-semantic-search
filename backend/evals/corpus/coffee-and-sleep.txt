Coffee and sleep

From the talk of the sleep doctor at work.

Caffeine has a half-life of about five to six hours. A coffee at 4 pm still has
half of its caffeine in the blood at 10 pm. You may fall asleep, but the deep
sleep is shorter.

Her advice:
- The last coffee before 2 pm, for someone who goes to bed at 11.
- Tea and cola also have caffeine. Decaf has very little.
- The first coffee better 60 to 90 minutes after you wake up, not at once.
- More than four coffees a day is too much for most adults.

She said that coffee is not bad for sleep in the morning; the problem is only
the coffee of the afternoon.
