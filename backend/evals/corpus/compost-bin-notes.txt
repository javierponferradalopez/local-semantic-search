Compost bin notes

The bin is the green 400 litre one in the corner of the plot.

What goes in:
- Greens: vegetable peels, coffee grounds, fresh grass, the weeds before they
  make seeds.
- Browns: dry leaves, straw, cardboard without ink, small sticks.
- About two buckets of browns for each bucket of greens.

What does not go in: meat, fish, dairy, cooked food with oil, sick plants, and
cat or dog waste.

Turn it with the fork every two weeks. It must be damp like a sponge that you
squeeze: if it smells bad, add browns; if it is dry, add water.

It is ready in about four months in summer and six in winter: dark, crumbly, and
it smells of forest. Then I sieve it and spread it on the beds in February.
