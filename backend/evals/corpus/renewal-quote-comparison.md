The renewal letter came in April. The cover stays the same as today; only the
price changes, so this note compares prices.

| Insurer | Cover | Price per year | Excess |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current company (renewal) | fully comprehensive | 589 € | 300 € |
| Current company (this year) | fully comprehensive | 512 € | 300 € |
| Online insurer A | fully comprehensive | 545 € | 450 € |
| Online insurer B | third party plus | 470 € | none |

## What I think

- The current company goes up 77 euros, with no claim of ours. I will call them
  and ask them to keep the price, and say that insurer A offers 545 euros.
- Insurer A is cheaper, but its excess is 450 euros, and one small dent eats the
  difference.
- Third party plus is too little for a car of four years.

Decision: call before 15 May. If they do not lower the price, change to
insurer A.
