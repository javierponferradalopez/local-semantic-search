# Bathroom renovation ideas

Ideas for the small bathroom, for after the kitchen.

## Shower

Replace the bathtub with a walk-in shower with a fixed glass panel and no door.
A floor drain along the wall lets the floor tiles run without a step.

## Tiles

Large tiles of 60 by 120 cm on the floor and the walls, so there are few joints
to clean. A niche in the shower wall holds the bottles.

## Details

- A heated towel rail instead of the radiator.
- A wall-hung toilet, which makes the floor easy to clean.
- A mirror cabinet with a light, so the small room has more storage.
