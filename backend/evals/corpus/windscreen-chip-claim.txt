Windscreen chip: the claim

A stone hit the windscreen on the motorway on Friday. It left a chip the size of
a coin, on the passenger side.

I called the assistance line of the insurer on Saturday. They gave me an
appointment with the glass company for Monday, at home, in the garage.

The technician filled the chip with resin in half an hour. He said that a chip
smaller than a two euro coin and out of the view of the driver can be repaired;
a larger one means a new windscreen.

I paid nothing. The policy covers the glass with no excess, and a repair does not
count as a claim, so the price of the renewal does not go up.

Claim number: 0042-7781.
