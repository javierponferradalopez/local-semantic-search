# Red lentil soup for the week

One big pot on Sunday gives five lunches. The soup is vegetarian and costs very
little.

## Ingredients

- 400 g of red split lentils
- One onion, two carrots and a stick of celery
- One teaspoon of ground cumin and a pinch of chilli
- A tin of chopped tomatoes
- 1.5 litres of vegetable stock and half a lemon

## Method

Lentils never need soaking, unlike chickpeas or dried beans. Rinse them in a
sieve until the water runs clear, which takes about a minute.

Soften the onion, the carrots and the celery in olive oil for ten minutes, add
the cumin and the chilli, then the lentils, the tomatoes and the stock. Red
lentils cook in twenty minutes and fall apart, which is what this soup wants.

To make it thicker, blend half of the pot and stir it back in, or mash a boiled
potato into it. If it gets too thick on the second day, add a splash of stock.
Finish each bowl with a squeeze of lemon.

## Storing

Cooked lentils keep for four days in the fridge in a closed container. The soup
freezes well for up to three months: freeze it in single portions, leave two
centimetres of space at the top of each box, and thaw it overnight in the
fridge.
