# Lisbon in four days

The plan I sent to friends who go to Lisbon next month.

## Day 1: Alfama and the castle

Walk up through Alfama to the Castelo de São Jorge before ten, when the queue
is short. Have dinner in a small tasca in the old streets.

## Day 2: tram 28 and Belém

Take tram 28 across the old town. Board it at the first stop, Martim Moniz, or
it is packed after two stops. In the afternoon, take the train to Belém for the
Tower, the Jerónimos Monastery and the pastéis de nata: custard tarts that you
dust with cinnamon and icing sugar at the table.

## Day 3: Sintra

Sintra is the best day trip from Lisbon. The train leaves from Rossio station
every twenty minutes and takes forty minutes. Book a timed ticket for the Pena
Palace online, and walk down to the Quinta da Regaleira after lunch.

## Day 4: LX Factory and the river

Breakfast at LX Factory, then walk along the river to Cais do Sodré.

## Getting around

Buy a Viva Viagem card at any metro station. You top it up with money, and it
works on the metro, the trams, the trains to Belém and Sintra, and the ferry to
Cacilhas.
