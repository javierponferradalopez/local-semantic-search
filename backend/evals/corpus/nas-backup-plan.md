# NAS backup plan

The NAS in the office cupboard holds the family photos and the backups of the
laptops.

## The disks

Two 4 TB disks in RAID 1: each disk is a copy of the other, so one disk can
fail without data loss. RAID is not a backup: a deleted file is deleted on
both disks.

## The backups

- Each laptop backs up to the NAS every night at 2:00.
- The phones upload their photos to the NAS when they charge at home.
- Snapshots of the photo folder are kept for thirty days, so a deleted photo
  can be restored.

## The copy outside the house

On the first Sunday of each month, copy the photo folder to an external drive
and take it to my parents' house. Three copies, two kinds of media, one copy in
another place.
