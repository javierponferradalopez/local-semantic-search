Switching the electricity supplier

Notes from the call with the new company, so that we do it right.

- Switching is free. The old company cannot charge us for leaving, because our
  contract has no minimum stay.
- The light is never cut off. The same cables and the same meter stay; only the
  company that sends the bill changes.
- We only need the ID of the account holder, the bank account and the CUPS code.
  The CUPS is a code of 20 or 22 characters that starts with ES. It is printed
  on the first page of every bill, near the address of the supply.
- The new company does all the paperwork with the old one. We do not have to call
  the old company at all.
- The change takes about 21 days. The old company sends one last bill for the
  days up to the change.

Signed online on 3 February. Expected start: around 24 February.
