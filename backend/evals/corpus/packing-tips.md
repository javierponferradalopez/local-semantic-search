# Packing tips for the move

## The boxes

- Heavy things in small boxes, light things in big boxes. A big box of books is
  too heavy to lift.
- Books flat or with the spine down, never with the pages down, in small boxes.
- Plates on their edge, like records, with paper between them.
- Fill the gaps with towels or paper, so that nothing moves.
- Write the room and the number on two sides of each box, not on the top.

## Who packs what

The movers only pack the fragile things: the glasses, the mirror, the lamps and
the TV. The rest we pack ourselves, in the boxes that they bring a week before.

## The first-night box

One box that travels with us in the car, not in the truck: the kettle, two mugs,
tea and coffee, the phone chargers, toilet paper, a towel each, the medicines, a
change of clothes, the sheets for the bed and a screwdriver.
