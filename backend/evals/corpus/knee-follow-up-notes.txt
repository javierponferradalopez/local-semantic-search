Knee follow-up notes, six-week review

Pain has gone down from 6 out of 10 to 2 out of 10. No locking and no swelling
since week three. Full range of movement.

The partial tear of the medial meniscus is healing with the physiotherapy.
Surgery is not needed. An arthroscopy would only be considered if the knee
locks again.

Plan: continue the exercises at home three times a week. Light running on flat
ground from now on. Return to sports with pivoting and sudden turns at three
months from the injury.

Next review only if the symptoms come back.
