# Rock pools with the kids

Our favourite morning of the holiday in the north. Notes for next summer.

## When to go

Look at the tide table the day before. Arrive one hour before low tide, and leave
the rocks when the tide starts to come back in. The rocks with the green weed are
very slippery: shoes with a rubber sole, never flip-flops.

## What we found

- Shore crabs under the stones. Lift the stone, look, and put it back the same way
  up.
- Red beadlet anemones. In the water they open like flowers; out of the water
  they close like a jelly sweet.
- Limpets and periwinkles on every rock.
- A small blenny fish that hid under the weed.
- Prawns in the deep pools: almost invisible until they move.

## The rules for the kids

- Look with the eyes and the bucket, and put each animal back where it was.
- Never pull a limpet from its rock: it cannot hold on again and dies.
- The bucket in the shade, with water from the same pool, for ten minutes at most.
