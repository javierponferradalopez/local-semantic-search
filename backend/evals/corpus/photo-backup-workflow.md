# How I keep my photos

After each trip I follow the same steps, so that no photo is lost.

1. Copy the card into the "Fotos/Import" folder of the laptop.
2. Import into Lightroom. Keywords: the place and the people.
3. Delete the bad photos the same week, while I still remember them.
4. Format the card in the camera, never on the laptop.

## The 3-2-1 rule

- Three copies: the laptop, the external disk and the cloud.
- On two kinds of media: the internal disk and the external disk.
- One copy out of the house: the cloud folder of my account, which syncs at
  night.

The external disk is the blue 2 TB one, in the drawer of the desk. Once a month I
plug it in and the backup app copies only the new files.

The original RAW files are kept forever. The exported JPEG files only for one
year, because Lightroom can make them again.
