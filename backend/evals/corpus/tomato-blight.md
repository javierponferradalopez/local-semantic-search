# Blight on the tomatoes

Last August the tomato plants got blight (mildiu) in one week. Notes so that it
does not happen again.

## How it looks

- Brown spots on the leaves, with a yellow edge, first on the lower leaves.
- A white fuzz under the leaf when the morning is wet.
- Brown, hard patches on the green tomatoes.

## What to do

1. Cut the leaves with spots at once and put them in the bin, never in the
   compost.
2. Spray with copper (Bordeaux mixture). The rules of the allotment forbid
   chemical sprays; only the products that organic farming allows, and copper is
   one of them.
3. Remove the lower leaves up to 30 cm from the soil, so that the rain does not
   splash the soil onto them.

## How to stop it

- Water at the base of the plant and in the morning, never on the leaves and
  never in the evening.
- Leave 60 cm between the plants so that the air moves.
- Do not grow tomatoes where the potatoes were last year.
