# My V60 recipe

The pour-over that I make every morning, for one big cup.

## The numbers

- 15 g of coffee for 250 g of water: a ratio of 1 to 16.6.
- Water at 93 °C: 30 seconds off the boil. Too hot makes it bitter, too cold makes
  it sour.
- A medium-fine grind, like table salt. For the moka pot I grind a little finer,
  like fine sand.
- Filtered water. The tap water here is hard: the coffee tastes flat, and the
  kettle fills with scale.

## The pour

1. Rinse the paper filter with hot water and throw that water away.
2. Bloom: 40 g of water, wait 30 seconds, so that the gas leaves the coffee.
3. Pour to 150 g in slow circles, then to 250 g.
4. The water must be through at about 3 minutes. Longer means the grind is too
   fine; shorter means it is too coarse.

## The beans

Fresh beans from the roaster. I let them rest one week after the roast date
before I open the bag, and I finish them before one month.
