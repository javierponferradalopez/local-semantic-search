# Solar panels for the roof: the quote

The installer came on Tuesday to look at the roof. The south side gets sun all
day, with no shade from the trees.

## What they offer

- 8 panels of 450 W, so 3.6 kWp in total.
- One inverter, with an app to see the production.
- No battery. The energy that we do not use goes to the grid, and the company pays
  it back on the bill ("compensación de excedentes").
- Price: 5,900 euros with the installation and the paperwork.
- The town hall gives back 50 % of the property tax (IBI) for three years.

## Will it pay back?

They estimate that the panels give 5,200 kWh a year, and that we use about 60 %
of it at home. With the price of today, that saves about 800 euros a year, so the
panels pay for themselves in about 7 years. They last 25.

The contracted power of 4.6 kW stays as it is; the panels do not change it.

We still have to decide if we want a battery. With one, the price goes up to
9,400 euros.
