# Chickpea hummus

A creamy hummus from dried chickpeas, not from a tin.

## Soaking and cooking

Dried chickpeas must soak for twelve hours in plenty of cold water with half a
teaspoon of baking soda. Drain them, cover them with fresh water and simmer for
about an hour and a half, until one crushes easily between two fingers.

## Blending

Blend the warm chickpeas with four tablespoons of tahini, the juice of one
lemon, one clove of garlic and a pinch of salt. Add ice water one spoonful at a
time while the blender runs: the cold water makes the hummus pale and smooth.

Serve it with olive oil, sweet paprika and warm flatbread. It keeps for five
days in the fridge, covered with a thin layer of oil.
