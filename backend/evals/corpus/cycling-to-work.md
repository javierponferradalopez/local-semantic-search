# Cycling to work

Eight kilometres each way, four days a week, since March.

## The route

The fastest route follows the main avenue, but the bike lane by the river is
only three minutes slower and has no traffic lights. In winter it is dark at
six, so the river route is better lit than the side streets.

## What I carry

- Two waterproof panniers: one for the laptop, one for clothes.
- A D-lock and a cable for the front wheel. The bike stays in the office
  garage, but a cheap lock is not enough on the street.
- A rain jacket and overshoes from October to April.

## At the office

There is a shower on the ground floor. I leave a towel, shoes and a shirt for
the week on Monday, so the panniers stay light.
