# Kitchen design decisions

Why we chose what we chose for the new kitchen.

## Induction, not gas

The induction hob heats a pan of water in half the time, the glass is cold
around the pan, and it cleans with one wipe. We also remove the gas line, so
there is no gas inspection every five years.

## Quartz, not granite

Quartz does not absorb stains, so oil and wine wipe off and it never needs
sealing. Granite is porous and must be sealed every year. Quartz also has a
uniform colour, so the joint between two pieces is almost invisible.

## The layout

An L-shape along the two longest walls, so the table fits by the window. The
sink is under the window and is fixed under the countertop, so crumbs go
straight in.

## The light

LED strips under the wall cabinets light the countertop, and a warm pendant
hangs over the table.
