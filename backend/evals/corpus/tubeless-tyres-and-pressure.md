# Tubeless tyres and pressure

Notes for the road bike with 28 mm tubeless tyres.

## Pressure

Tyres lose air overnight, so check the pressure with a track pump before every
ride. The right pressure depends on the weight of the rider:

| Rider weight | Front | Rear |
|---|---|---|
| under 65 kg | 5.0 bar | 5.5 bar |
| 65 to 80 kg | 5.5 bar | 6.0 bar |
| over 80 kg | 6.0 bar | 6.5 bar |

On wet or broken roads, take off 0.3 bar for more grip.

## Sealant

The liquid sealant inside the tyre dries out with time. Top it up with 30 ml
every two to three months, and more often in a hot summer. Shake the bottle
and inject it through the valve with the core removed.

## A puncture on the road

1. Stop, find the hole and turn it to the bottom of the wheel, so that the
   sealant can reach it. Spin the wheel for a few seconds.
2. If air still escapes, push a tubeless plug into the hole with the plug tool,
   and cut the end of the plug flush with the tyre.
3. Inflate again with a CO2 cartridge or a mini pump.
4. If the cut is too big for a plug, remove the valve and fit a spare inner
   tube.
