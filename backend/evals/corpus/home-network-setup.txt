Home network setup

The fibre line comes in at the hall, so the ISP router sits there. It is in
bridge mode: it only turns the fibre signal into Ethernet, and the mesh system
does the routing and the wifi.

There are three mesh nodes: one in the hall, one in the living room and one in
the upstairs office. The office node is wired back to the hall with a Cat6
cable through the stairwell, so video calls do not depend on the wireless
backhaul.

The main network uses WPA3. Guests join a separate SSID that cannot see the
printer or the NAS. The smart plugs and the thermostat live on a third network
for IoT devices, because many of them only speak 2.4 GHz.

If the internet drops, restart in this order: the ISP router first, wait two
minutes, then the main mesh node. The other nodes reconnect on their own.
